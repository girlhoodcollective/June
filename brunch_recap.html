Better Together Brunch

Thank You for
Being Part of It

This year's Better Together Brunch reminded us what happens when women gather with purpose.

Together, our community came together through ticket sales, sponsorships, and support — with more than $1,000 going directly to Endurance in Education, our nonprofit partner for this event.

From meaningful conversations and new friendships to inspiring speakers and local business partnerships, the morning reflected exactly what Girlhood Collective exists to create: spaces where women feel connected, supported, and empowered.

Thank you to every attendee, sponsor, volunteer, and community partner who made this event possible.
