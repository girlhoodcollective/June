About Endurance in Education

"A structured mentorship ecosystem where investing in our youth creates a ripple effect of success across our entire community."

EIE's P.O.D. (Pursue · Overcome · Develop) model connects high school students, college students, and workforce mentors in cross-industry mentorship groups. Students build confidence, explore career paths, and develop real professional skills. Mentors continue to grow as leaders. The result? A stronger talent pipeline for every Cincinnati business that calls this city home.

P.O.D. Stage 1

High School · Pursue

9th grade entry. Students identify interests, build confidence, set goals, and explore career paths.

P.O.D. Stage 2

College · Overcome

Students refine professional habits, gain leadership experience, and mentor those behind them.

P.O.D. Stage 3

Workforce · Develop

Mentors give back, stay connected to their community, and never stop learning and leading.
